Word Work – icon proposals

Five original characters in the Franco-Belgian “ligne claire” style: even black outlines, flat colours, 1950s–60s fashion. Each is drawn on the macOS icon grid and shown at Dock and menu sizes on light and dark backgrounds. Pick one (or mix features) and I’ll refine it and generate all app icons from it.

1 · Hélène

The novelist. Glossy dark bob with a straight fringe, cat-eye glasses and a pencil tucked behind the ear. Black turtleneck, teal background – the most “writer at work” of the five, and the clearest silhouette at small sizes.

2 · Margit

The columnist. Tall auburn beehive, pearl necklace and earrings, a burgundy cardigan over a white blouse on warm mustard. Very 1960s, friendly and confident.

3 · Simone

The reporter. Red beret, short dark curls, a sailor-striped shirt and a red scarf – with a wink. The most playful one; strong red/blue contrast reads well in the Dock.

4 · Ingrid

The one at the typewriter. In profile, polka-dot headscarf and blonde ponytail, typing on a green typewriter with a sheet of paper. Tells the story best at large size, but has the most detail – weakest at 16–32 px.

5 · Agnes

The grande dame. Silver bun, round glasses, smile lines and a quill. Olive cardigan on violet – the wise, experienced author.
